Chloe Presents · website structure

Site map

How the website is organised, and how the five homepage designs (V5, V2, V1, V6 and V7) guide visitors to each part. All five share the same pages and menu. Only the homepage layout differs.

Full page Side panel (opens over the page) Pop-up form
01 · The whole site

Eight destinations, four real pages

The menu offers eight places to go, so the site feels complete. Only the areas that will keep growing are full pages. Everything else opens over the current page, so visitors never lose their place. Close, Esc or Back always returns them to where they were.

HomeThe homepage: one of five designs

For visitors who want to discover the work.

ProjectsAll projects with category filters, plus a project explorer that links projects by theme.
