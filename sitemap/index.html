How the website is organised, and how the two homepage designs (V5 and V2) guide visitors to each part. Both designs share the same pages and menu. Only the homepage layout differs.

01 · The whole site

Eight destinations, four real pages

The menu offers eight places to go, so the site feels complete. Only the areas that will keep growing are full pages. Everything else opens over the current page, so visitors never lose their place. Close, Esc or Back always returns them to where they were.

04 · About this preview

Reading the preview

Stage

This is an early structure preview, between a wireframe and the final design. It shows layout and flow, not finished content.

Placeholders

  • Grey boxes stand in for photos and video.
  • Placeholder text and grey bars stand in for final wording.
  • Numbers and dates show as 0000.
  • Sound is switched off. The controls only show where it will go.

What you can click

  • Only the homepage is included.
  • About opens as a panel.
  • Links to other pages, panels and outside sites are paused in this preview.
